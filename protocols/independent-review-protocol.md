# Independent Review Protocol

## Purpose

This protocol tests whether unaffiliated reviewers can apply the autonomy profile, trust evidence register, and practical human control test using public instructions. It measures documentary agreement and exposes ambiguity. It does not estimate real-world safety or institutional effectiveness.

## Eligibility

A reviewer is eligible when the person:

- did not author the case packet or the repository version under review;
- did not participate in the target decision;
- declares relevant professional, financial, organizational, and personal conflicts;
- completes the review without private interpretive assistance from the author;
- preserves the initial result before adjudication.

## Minimum study design

Use at least three cases and three eligible reviewers. Cases should contain different evidence states:

1. evidence supports a bounded reliance claim;
2. evidence is incomplete or internally inconsistent;
3. evidence indicates that formal authority lacked practical force.

Freeze the repository commit, protocol version, case packets, expected output schema, and analysis plan before reviewers begin.

## Case packet requirements

Each packet should contain:

- a case identifier and scope statement;
- system and agent identity information;
- task, permissions, tools, and prohibited actions;
- event chronology;
- decision record and available evidence;
- monitor and alert records;
- authority and delegation records;
- intervention and downstream-effect records;
- appeal, correction, and repair records;
- declared omissions and inaccessible sources;
- provenance for each included file.

Remove expected classifications from the reviewer packet.

## Reviewer procedure

1. Record the repository commit and protocol version.
2. Declare conflicts and prior familiarity.
3. Review the packet without contacting the author.
4. Classify all six autonomy variables with cited packet locations.
5. Evaluate all twelve evidence propositions.
6. Evaluate all nine practical-control stages.
7. Record missing evidence, ambiguity, and protocol deviations.
8. State the bounded assurance proposition, if any, supported by the packet.
9. State conclusions that the packet cannot support.
10. Seal and submit the initial result before discussion.

## Required reporting

Publish or archive:

- frozen materials and cryptographic checksums;
- reviewer eligibility and conflicts;
- initial individual results;
- agreement and disagreement by item;
- missing-data treatment;
- adjudication records kept separate from initial results;
- reviewer comments on ambiguity and usability;
- protocol deviations;
- author response and resulting repository changes;
- inaccessible materials and publication constraints.

## Interpretation

Exact or high agreement on a small case set supports only a bounded implementability claim. Disagreement may identify construct ambiguity, insufficient instructions, missing evidence, or genuine judgment variation. Adjudicated agreement cannot replace the preserved initial results.

## Failure conditions

The review does not support an independent application claim when:

- reviewers receive private interpretive coaching;
- case materials or expected results change after review begins;
- initial results are overwritten during adjudication;
- disagreements or exclusions are omitted;
- reviewers helped design the version under test;
- only successful cases are reported.
