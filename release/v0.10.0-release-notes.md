# v0.10.0: Evidence-Gate Protocol Checkpoint

## Why release this checkpoint now

The repository completed all 89 author-screening decisions in v0.9. That result exposed a second problem: screening relevance and manuscript support require different evidence. Of the 27 retained-close sources, 8 have a recorded full-text review basis and 19 remain supported by title and abstract.

Version 0.10.0 freezes the next rules before those 19 decisions and the wider search-coverage results are known. The release therefore supplies a dated, inspectable record of the method that will govern the next evidence cycle.

## What this release adds

- A 27-source full-text ledger with 8 verified and 19 open records.
- Terminal states that separate verified full text, quarantined abstract-only use, post-review exclusion, and inaccessible sources.
- A recovery protocol for all 1,087 records that lacked abstracts in the formal search.
- A reproducible 284-record residual-risk sample when unresolved records remain.
- Accountable logs for Scopus or Web of Science, ACM Digital Library, IEEE Xplore, PhilPapers, and HeinOnline or Harvard HOLLIS.
- A discovery log recording eight findings that changed the research sequence while preserving the three topics and frozen project question.
- An executable validator and a SHA-256 manifest sealing 154 research artifacts.

## Evidence boundary

This release establishes protocol fixation, source-state accounting, executable gate behavior, and artifact integrity for the declared checkpoint. It establishes no completed full-text review, exhaustive search coverage, independent reliability, field validity, institutional effectiveness, universal originality, or improved outcome claim.

The released v0.9 claim audit remains immutable. A v0.10 claim audit will follow after the new evidence gates advance.

## Next action

The next cycle will verify or quarantine the 19 retained-close sources, then begin recovery of the 1,087 inaccessible records under the frozen protocol.
