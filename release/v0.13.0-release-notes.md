# v0.13.0: Forward-Citation Author Screening

## What changed

This release closes the author-screening gate for the 71 forward citations whose content was recovered in v0.12.0. The decisions retain 13 close sources and 22 background sources, exclude 11 single-component sources and 25 off-topic sources, and preserve a record-level reason for every decision.

## Why it matters

The repository can now distinguish three separate questions: whether a source was recovered, whether it belongs in the research corpus, and whether it supports a manuscript proposition. The first two questions are closed for this 71-record queue. The third remains open. No retained source has claim permission until a separate locator-level review connects an exact proposition to inspected evidence.

## Evidence controls

- The screening protocol was fixed before the decisions were recorded.
- The input queue hash, membership, order, and 71 decisions are checked by executable controls.
- Every row records its rationale, inspected basis, source locator, decision owner, date, assistance disclosure, and claim-permission state.
- The claim map contains 28 material claims.
- The integrity audit detects all 26 prespecified corruptions and publishes four exceptions.

## Limits

The result is an author-authorized, AI-assisted single-assessor screening pass. It supplies no inter-assessor reliability estimate, proposition-level support from the retained sources, close-source prevalence estimate, completed multidisciplinary search, originality finding, institutional effect, or transfer result. Another 980 retrieval outcomes, one direct-query screening decision, backward-reference retrieval, authenticated database searches, and retained-source proposition review remain open.

## Review state
