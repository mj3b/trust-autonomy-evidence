# v0.11.0: Direct-Query Evidence and Claim-Control Checkpoint

## Why this release matters

Version 0.11.0 applies the claim controls introduced in v0.5.0 to the newest research work. It does not treat a recovered source, a screening decision, or a plausible comparison as a conclusion merely because it appears in the repository.

The release separates six questions: Was the source recovered? What part was inspected? What decision did the author record? Which bounded claim does the evidence support? Is the evidence fit for that exact claim? Do the prerequisites close before the claim enters a conclusion?

## What this release adds

- A frozen 284-record residual-risk sample drawn from the 1,087-record recovery population.
- Five direct-query retrieval outcomes with route, locator, review basis, source observation, screening state, decision owner, assistance disclosure, and limits.
- Four recorded screening decisions: three background sources and one close source. One decision remains open.
- A 60-source literature matrix and an updated contribution audit.
- A human-review attestation covering the five direct-query records and six added claims.
- A deterministic 26-claim evidence map derived from the preserved v0.9 map.
- A v0.11 integrity audit that detects all 22 controlled corruptions.
- Four published exceptions covering independent assessment, incomplete search coverage, two source limits, and institutional transfer.
- A SHA-256 manifest sealing 171 research artifacts.

## Audit result

The audit returns `PASS_WITH_EXCEPTIONS`. The five-record workflow claim and four bounded source descriptions pass the declared gates. `PAPER-C32`, a proposed transfer from a model-level residual-path result to a wider control mechanism, remains conclusion-ineligible because independence and completeness are indeterminate.

A passing control means the repository detected the corruption it was designed to detect. It does not mean a source is true, the method is independently reliable, or an institution will obtain a better outcome.

## Standing research rule

Exploration may begin with incomplete notes. Once data, a source observation, a figure, or a finding is used to support a material claim or release statement, it must enter the claim register and pass traceability, integrity, human support review, evidence fitness, and dependency closure. Failed or indeterminate required gates must remain visible and must block the affected conclusion.
