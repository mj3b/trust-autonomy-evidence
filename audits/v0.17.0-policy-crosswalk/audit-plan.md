# v0.17.0 Policy Crosswalk Audit Plan

## Decision

The audit decides whether the proposed policy extension can be reviewed without changing the v0.16.0 historical findings or implying legal compliance.

## Inputs

- eight-record official-source review;
- policy and standards crosswalk;
- prospective regulatory-sandbox protocol;
- five-claim extension map;
- nine controlled mutations;
- current practical-human-control protocol.

## Checks

1. verify the declared source count;
2. detect specification and scope violations;
3. resolve every local evidence locator and digest;
4. compare the six-stage sandbox method with the validator;
5. test evidence fitness and dependency closure;
6. verify that every manuscript-use claim resolves to the recorded author attestation and retains its legal and empirical limits.

## Acceptance rule

The candidate passes with exceptions when all nine mutations are detected, every evidence path resolves, every fitness and dependency check passes, and bounded author approval resolves to the recorded attestation. Any escaped mutation, missing evidence file, unsupported dependency, or compliance claim fails the audit.
