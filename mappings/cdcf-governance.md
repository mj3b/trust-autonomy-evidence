# Mapping: CDCF Governance

## Relationship

The [Catholic Digital Commons Foundation governance repository](https://github.com/CatholicOS/foundation-docs) contains project-vetting criteria, lifecycle procedures, committee structures, and research material. It provides an institutional setting in which governance requirements may influence project acceptance, incubation, graduation, maintenance, and retirement.

## Evidence relationship

| Trust mechanism | Potential CDCF evidence | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Institutional responsibility | Named committees, roles, lifecycle stages, and review criteria | Published structure does not establish consistent practice |
| Constraint acceptance | Gate criteria can delay, reject, or condition a project | A gate has force only when failure changes a decision |
| Accountability | Project roles, governance obligations, maintenance, and retirement procedures | Assigned responsibility does not establish completed action |
| Learning | Versioned policies and community review | Version change requires a traceable link to evidence or experience |
| Independence | Review by people outside the submitting project | Organizational separation does not guarantee impartial judgment |

## Claim boundary

Institutional uptake is evidence that an artifact entered a governance process. It does not establish operational adoption, policy effectiveness, harm prevention, or conformity with external requirements. Future case work should preserve the submitted evidence, reviewer findings, decision, conditions, dissent, and later outcomes.
