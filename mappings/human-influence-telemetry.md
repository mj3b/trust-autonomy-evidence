# Mapping: Human Influence Telemetry

## Relationship

[Human Influence Telemetry](https://github.com/mj3b/human-influence-telemetry) assesses whether available records support a documentary claim that human authority retained practical force in an AI-mediated decision.

Trust, Autonomy, and Evidence places that assessment inside a broader reliance model. HIT addresses parts of access, authority, exercise, effect, and repair. The broader model also requires agent scope, capability, reliability, uncertainty, monitoring, integrity, and institutional learning evidence.

## Evidence relationship

| Practical-control stage | Potential HIT evidence | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Access | Evidence available to the human before commitment | Availability does not prove comprehension |
| Authority | Formal decision and intervention rights | Assigned authority may be infeasible to exercise |
| Exercise | Recorded human action or independent judgment | Documentary evidence cannot reveal every cognitive process |
| Effect | Downstream change linked to human action | Local effect may not propagate across every dependent system |
| Correction and repair | Appeal, reversal, remediation, and assigned obligations | Procedures do not establish completed remedy |

## Claim boundary

HIT conformance concerns the published documentary rubric. It does not establish correctness, legality, fairness, accuracy, safety, or moral justification. Findings from a HIT release require their own evidence and cannot transfer automatically to this repository.
