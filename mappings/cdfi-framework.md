# Mapping: CDFI Framework

## Relationship

The [CDFI Framework](https://github.com/mj3b/cdfi-framework) provides evaluation-governance infrastructure for domain-specific AI doctrinal benchmarking. Its relevance here is methodological: evaluation claims depend on scoring contracts, human reviewer reliability, publication gates, and explicit limits.

## Evidence relationship

| Trust mechanism | Potential CDFI evidence | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Competence | Defined evaluation method, prompts, metrics, and traceability | A detailed method may still measure the wrong construct |
| Reliability | Human and automated scoring consistency checks | Reliability does not establish validity |
| Candor | Failed gates, weak agreement, and unresolved results remain visible | Disclosure does not resolve the failure |
| Constraint acceptance | Publication claims are limited when acceptance conditions fail | A declared gate requires evidence that it changed publication behavior |
| Learning | Versioned changes respond to evaluation findings | Revision does not establish improvement without retesting |

## Claim boundary

CDFI is an adjacent evaluation-governance case. Its metrics and domain conclusions do not transfer into a general account of trustworthy autonomous systems.
