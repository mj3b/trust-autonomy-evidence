# Mapping: Governed Decision Intelligence

## Relationship

[Governed Decision Intelligence](https://github.com/mj3b/governed-decision-intelligence) defines a schema-validated record for a consequential AI-assisted decision. The record includes the decision question, system output, evidence, alternatives, risk posture, authority, gate classification, and downstream obligations.

Trust, Autonomy, and Evidence supplies a separate evaluative layer. It asks what bounded reliance proposition those records may support and which evidence remains missing.

## Evidence relationship

| Trust proposition | Potential GDI evidence | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Identity is known | Agent, model, owner, deployer, and authority identifiers | Recorded identity still requires verification |
| Evidence is sufficiently complete | Evidence manifest, completeness statement, and gaps | A complete record can contain false or irrelevant evidence |
| Human authority has force | Named decision authority, delegation, gate, and escalation | Formal authority does not establish feasible or exercised intervention |
| Decisions are reconstructable | Question, alternatives, uncertainty, outcome, and obligations | Reconstruction does not establish decision quality |
| Integrity is preserved | Sealed fields and modification detection | Integrity does not establish original truthfulness |
| Governance changes under evidence | Reassessment and downstream obligations | Recorded obligations do not establish completion |

## Claim boundary

GDI can supply decision evidence to this project. Neither repository establishes that a complete record preserves substantive human judgment, satisfies a governance requirement, or improves outcomes.
