# Changelog

All material changes to concepts, claims, protocols, and evidence requirements are recorded here.

## 0.1.0, 7 August 2026

Initial working research release.

- Defines trust, trustworthiness, reliance, justified reliance, and calibration.
- Defines a six-variable autonomy profile.
- Defines a seven-level trust evidence ladder.
- Publishes a twelve-proposition trust evidence register.
- Publishes draft protocols for independent review and practical human control assessment.
- Maps the research program to GDI, HIT, CDFI, and CDCF governance artifacts.
- Records current claims, limitations, and empirical acceptance conditions.
- Adds executable validation for required files, internal links, version alignment, claim identifiers, and the public/private boundary.
