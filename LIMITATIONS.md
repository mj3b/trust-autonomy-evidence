# Limitations and Threats to Validity

## Construct validity

The project operationalizes justified reliance and practical human control through observable records. Documentary evidence may omit informal pressure, misunderstanding, concealed incentives, unrecorded actions, or false entries. A complete record can therefore coexist with ceremonial review or ineffective authority.

The six autonomy variables may omit properties that matter in a particular domain. Interactions among variables may also matter more than any individual score.

## Internal validity

The author selected the initial concepts, sources, propositions, and boundaries. This creates risks of confirmation, source-selection, and category-design bias. Public traceability permits scrutiny but does not remove those risks.

Any case prepared after an outcome is known may contain hindsight reconstruction. Prospective record creation and frozen review packets can reduce this risk.

## Reliability

The repository has no completed multi-case reliability study for its current protocols. Clear definitions and examples do not establish consistent application by independent reviewers.

## External validity

The model has not been validated across sectors, cultures, legal systems, system architectures, or levels of consequence. Evidence sufficient for a reversible internal workflow may be inadequate for healthcare, employment, finance, public benefits, security, or other high-consequence settings.

## Operational validity

The presence of monitoring, review, appeal, or stop authority does not establish that those controls work under time pressure, staffing limits, organizational incentives, or irreversible execution. Operational claims require records of exercised controls and downstream effects.

## Integrity limits

Hashes, signatures, timestamps, and protected logs can support integrity, attribution, and ordering. They do not establish the truth, completeness, relevance, or fairness of the recorded content.

## Legal and normative limits

Mappings to NIST, the EU AI Act, ISO standards, or other governance instruments can identify potentially relevant evidence. This repository does not provide legal advice, certification, conformity assessment, or a finding that a requirement has been satisfied.

## Outcome limits

The project has no evidence that its use improves decisions, prevents harm, increases justified trust, or produces effective repair. Those propositions require defined outcomes and suitable comparative evidence.
